The foundation, and what unlocks it

For the people already on Copilot, the foundation is a $9 step-up away.

+$9per user / month to E7
For our users who already have Copilot, moving to E7 is a $9 per user per month step-up, and it adds the full agent control plane (Agent 365) and identity governance (Entra) that our working model left out. We are already paying for the expensive part. The foundation completes it.

The debate is not Copilot versus E7. It is buying Copilot, Entra, and a control plane separately and later, versus one foundation that bundles them, with the step-up to E7 as little as $9 for people already on Copilot.

4 How we cover our people Coverage strategy

Lead with full coverage

Put all ~20,000 eligible information workers on the foundation, sized to our expected AI-adoption trajectory. One standard, everyone governed, no second migration later.

Have the phased alternative ready

Show lower-coverage options (for example the ~10,000 in our working model) so the board can choose the pace, with pricing that stays coherent across user profiles.

5 Managing consumption

Consumption is not eliminated by E7, and we do not claim it is. The question the board is really asking is how we forecast and control it. That starts with strategy, then mechanics.

First: the strategy
  • Tier the work. Foundation-covered for everyday scenarios; metered only for differentiated, autonomous, external, and advanced workloads.
  • Decide how, when, and why we consume before we scale, not after.
Then: the mechanics
  • Forecast with per-workload telemetry; budget caps by department with ownership.
  • Govern with approval gates; optimize through model routing and retiring waste.
The foundation is where consumption becomes governed and predictable instead of a PAYGO line item that grows one agent at a time, exactly the risk our single-agent model does not yet show.
